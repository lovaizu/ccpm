## What deliberately stopped being a criterion

Three clauses are now decisions rather than criteria, and can change without a criteria rewrite: the
physical two-layer separation (4.1, D-3), the 1,000–3,000 word / 500 line bound (D-9), and the
absence of mermaid in the prompt body (4.1). This is the point of the task — the shape of the prompt
is the design's business, and the criteria judge what a run achieves.
