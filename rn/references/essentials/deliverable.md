# Deliverable

Everything the session changed apart from its directory under `.rn/`. Its readers are the user, who
approves it at the Deliverable sign-off, and the next session, which starts from it. Each question
asks whether a part of the change does its job for the goal.

## What changed

- Does each line of Goal achieved when hold when the product is run?

    The user then has the goal they approved. With one line not holding, the goal is not achieved.

- Is the goal itself achieved, not only the words of each line?

    The user gets what they wanted, not only what was written down. The lines can be met while the
    goal is missed.

- Does it pass the checks the design document set?

    It is then judged by the measure set before it was built, which cannot be eased to fit what was
    built.

- Has the work shown nothing the goal needs that Goal achieved when leaves out?

    A gap seen now is decided by the user before approving. Left unseen, it is found in use.

## What did not change

- Does everything that worked before still work?

    The user keeps what they had. What breaks, they find in use and have to fix.

## What was added

- Is nothing added that the goal does not need?

    The user receives only what they approved. A change the goal does not need is theirs to find and
    remove.

- Does the whole change follow the Rules in `steering.md`?

    The change then fits the repository as its people expect. Each task can follow the Rules while
    the whole does not.

## The README and design document

- Do they say what was built?

    The next session then starts from ground that holds. Where they differ from what was built, the
    next work starts from a wrong one.
