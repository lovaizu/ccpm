# Changelog

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- rn proposes the goal it reads you're after, with the words of yours it rests on, the way to it, and what it makes, marking what it assumed, so you correct only what's off instead of answering one question at a time.
- rn asks nothing that your request, your issue, or your repository already says: a request whose result is settled takes one proposal, and one that leaves more open takes a few, the goal first.
- rn names each file it changes with what changes in it, and asks which new things you want to see as soon as they're made, waiting for your word on each before it makes what rests on it.
- rn answers your question with why, and changes nothing until you ask for a change.
- rn keeps your decisions in your own words.
- rn works on the branch you start it on, and asks you what to do where that branch isn't one for the work, such as the default branch. It makes a branch or a worktree only when you ask for one.
- On a cancel, rn asks what goes, and removes only what you confirm.
- A part that no best practice fits is made by the general kind, which holds what every part needs: who uses it and for what, nothing more than that needs, a check agreed before it's made, the form its reader takes, and no disagreement with your repository. Whether it's refactored as code or as a document is part of its shape that you agree to. It takes the place of the light kind.
- Before rn makes each part, it shows you the part's shape in your words, and makes it as you agree. Each best practice names what a part rests on as what it is, such as who it's for, never as another document, so a one-line fix needs no design document first.
- rn commits and pushes once each purpose ends, such as a part made or a step tried, each commit in the shape of your repository's, and opens its draft pull request in the shape of your recent ones.
- The need's tree is one flat list, and a solution no longer carries a riskiest assumption to test, as the least that runs in your hands tests it.

### Fixed

- A role's end moves on only the work of its own worktree, so work in another worktree never stops it or gets committed half written.
- rn removes the folders it tries a deliverable in once the try is over, and on a cancel.

### Removed

- The work's own best practices in `.rn/best-practices/`, which nothing let you reach.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- rn pushes its work to GitHub as it goes, so nothing stays only on your computer.
- Where AI evaluators still find something after two tries, rn asks you whether to mend it now, so you decide what you'd live with.
- Each deliverable is refactored by the best practice of its kind before you try it, and rn reviews its pull request as its author would before it proposes the merge, so reviewers meet a clean change.
- Where the way to make what you asked for changes, rn makes each thing again from its start, so nothing made the old way holds up your work.
- rn checks each way to make what you asked for, its license, upkeep, open issues, and standing, before it offers it, so you pick only among ways that hold.
- rn asks you before it makes something without the best practice of its kind, so what you try holds the quality its kind needs.
- rn works in a worktree you agree to, the one you started it in or one it makes as your repository's rules or your tools ask, so your own work stays as you left it.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-25
