# Changelog

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- rn shows in one message the goal in your own words, the way to it, and what it makes, marking what it assumed, so you correct only what's off instead of answering one question at a time.
- rn asks nothing that your request, your issue, or your repository already says, so a request whose result is settled goes to the proposal at once.
- rn keeps your words as you said them, so what it aims at doesn't turn your meaning around.
- rn asks which things it makes you want to see as soon as they're made, and waits for your word on each before it makes what rests on it.
- The one you talk with weighs each point and answers why at once, so nothing waits on another role.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- rn pushes its work to GitHub as it goes, so nothing stays only on your computer.
- Where AI evaluators still find something after two tries, rn asks you whether to mend it now, so you decide what you'd live with.
- Each deliverable is refactored by the best practice of its kind before you try it, and rn reviews its pull request as its author would before it proposes the merge, so reviewers meet a clean change.
- Where the way to make what you asked for changes, rn makes each thing again from its start, so nothing made the old way holds up your work.
- rn checks each way to make what you asked for, its license, upkeep, open issues, and standing, before it offers it, so you pick only among ways that hold.
- rn asks you before it makes something without the best practice of its kind, so what you try holds the quality its kind needs.
- rn works in a worktree you agree to, the one you started it in or one it makes as your repository's rules or your tools ask, so your own work stays as you left it.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-25

### Changed

- rn keeps only what your goal's people can't reach the goal without, and asks you only what your words and repository leave open.
- You sign off only the steps you choose to check, and you merge the pull request yourself.
- rn no longer needs Lane.

### Added

- An AI can start, pause, and pick up a run, so it can run rn in each worktree.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-22

### Changed

- You sign off a plan of the deliverables before rn makes any, and rn remakes each until its evaluators find it good for its people.
- rn works in its own worktree, so you keep working meanwhile.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-10-06

### Changed

- rn finds what you want behind your first words, and a user-like agent tries each result before you see it.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-07-07

### Added

- A session started under an older rn is brought up to date when you resume it.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-07-05

### Changed

- You sign off the plan, the approach, and the result, in place of each task.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-06-24
