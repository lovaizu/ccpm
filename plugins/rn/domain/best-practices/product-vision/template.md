## Objectives and key results

- `O<n>`: `<objective>`
  - `KR<n>.1`: `<key result>`. `<direction>`.
  - `KR<n>.2`: `<key result>`. `<direction>`.

`KR<n>.1` comes first: keep a change that improves a key result only if none before it gets
worse.
Key results have no targets, as a measure made a target stops being a good measure, by Goodhart's
law. They show whether a change brought its objective closer.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<objective>`: what one role of Roles is trying to achieve in their own work, in one sentence
>   begun in lowercase, as it follows a colon.
> - `<key result>`: what the developers measure of what the role does, in one sentence begun in
>   lowercase: `KR<n>.1` how well the role achieves the objective, and `KR<n>.2` how fast.
> - `<direction>`: which way is better, such as "Lower is better."

## Concept

`<Product>` `<the one way>`.

- `<idea>`, for `O<n>`\
  `<what it gives>`.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<the one way>`: how the product brings its users to their objectives, as its maker means it,
>   said as what the users see the product do, in one sentence that goes on from the product's
>   name.
> - `<idea>`: each idea that keeps the one way holding at a moment of the users' use, at most five,
>   each in a few words.
> - `<what it gives>`: what the idea gives the role toward the objective, in one short sentence.
