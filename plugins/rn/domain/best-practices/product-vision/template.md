## Roles

- `<role>`\
  `<who they are>`.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<role>`: each kind of person who comes to the product to get its job done, such as a guest, a

> - `<who they are>`: the people in the role and the job they hire the product for, in one clause begun in uppercase.

## Objectives and key results

- `O<n>`: `<objective>`
  - `KR<n>.<m>`: `<key result>`. `<direction>`.

The first key result of each objective comes first. Keep a change that improves a key result
only if none before it gets worse.
Key results have no targets, as a measure made a target stops being a good measure, by Goodhart's
law. They show whether a change brought its objective closer.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<objective>`: what one role of Roles is trying to achieve in their own work, in one sentence
>   begun in lowercase, as it follows a colon.
> - `<key result>`: each thing the developers measure of what the role does, in one sentence begun
>   in lowercase: `KR<n>.1` how well the role achieves the objective, and a `KR<n>.2` of how fast
>   only where the time decides the objective.
> - `<direction>`: which way is better, such as "Lower is better."

## Concept

`<Product>` `<the one way>`.

- `<idea>`, for `O<n>`\
  `<what it gives>`.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<the one way>`: how the product brings its users to their objectives, as the purpose and
>   intent that its maker gave lead to, said as what the users see the product do, in one sentence
>   that goes on from the product's name.
> - `<idea>`: each idea without which the one way fails at a moment of the users' use, in a few
>   words. Leave out the ideas where the one way holds without any.
> - `<what it gives>`: what the idea gives the role toward the objective, in one short sentence.
