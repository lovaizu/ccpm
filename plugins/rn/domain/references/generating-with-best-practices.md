# Generating with a best practice

A best practice holds what practitioners of a kind of deliverable know: the few states that bring
the deliverable's people to their aim, and the way that reaches each. Each of its parts is there
for one purpose. Use each for that purpose, in full, as a part read for less than its purpose
leaves the deliverable short where its people need it.

## `goal`

It says who uses the deliverable and what they achieve with it. Every choice you make serves that
achievement. Where no other part of the best practice settles a choice, take the one that brings
the goal's people closer to it.

## `critical_success_factors`

Each is one of the few things that must all hold for the people to reach the goal. Spend your
effort where a factor would otherwise fail, as that is where the people would fail.

## `necessary_conditions`

Each asks, yes or no, whether the deliverable holds a state that its factor needs. The deliverable
answers yes to every one, settled by the user's purpose and the steering's decisions, with what
the repository already holds that bears on it. An evaluator asks the same questions of the deliverable alone, so meet each
in what the deliverable says or does, not in how you made it.

- `about` names the section of the template where the people need the state. Meet the condition
  there, as that is where they look for it.
- `why` says what the condition gives the people, and the practitioners' way that the items come
  from. Decide from it each case that no item names.
- `items` are that way's decisions and steps, in order. Take each `decide` item before you write,
  so that the deliverable rests on choices made, and each `make` item as you write and once it's
  written, as that is where it checks what you wrote. An item that opens with `Where <case>` holds
  only in that case.

## `template`

It is the form that every deliverable of the kind takes, so that the people find each thing where
every other deliverable of the kind keeps it. Keep its fixed text as it stands, fill each slot in
the form its note gives, and remove each note once its slot is filled.
