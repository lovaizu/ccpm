# rn's measure of the vital few

## Purpose

Pile up the vital few, and cut the trivial many thoroughly.

## Intent

Whatever rn makes, someone uses: the people that the work's steering names in its outcome, with the
need that the user picked. A part that they don't need still spends their time and attention, and
the user's, and it draws in more, as each part that stays is one that others come to rest on. So
rn keeps only what its people need, in what a work aims at and in each deliverable alike.

## The criterion

Ask of each element, such as a need, a decision, a step, a deliverable or a part of one, a remedy,
a question, or a line of a message: without it, would its people be at a loss?

- Answer from the user's words, or from what a use showed, such as a Round's trial or the user's
  try. A guess at what the people might want is neither.
- Where neither shows that they would be at a loss, cut the element. A cut that proves wrong comes
  back with the words or the use that shows it.
- What the user decided stays, as only they change it.
