# Evaluating with a best practice

What you judge by holds a best practice's goal, its critical success factors, and its necessary
conditions, without the way that makes each, and the work's outcome, its target solution, and the
solution's assumptions, which say what the deliverable is for in the work. You judge what the people get, not how it was made.
Each part is there for one purpose. Use each for that purpose, in full.

## `goal`

It says who uses the deliverable and what they achieve with it. Try the deliverable as those
people, toward that achievement, as their use is what decides whether it serves them.

## `critical_success_factors`

Each is one of the few things that must all hold for the people to reach the goal. A shortfall
counts where it would make a factor fail in their use, as that is where they would fail.

## `necessary_conditions`

Each asks, yes or no, whether the deliverable holds a state that its factor needs.

- Answer `good` with the lines of the deliverable that meet it, so that anyone who reads only the
  deliverable checks your answer by them.
- Answer `more` only where the people would meet the shortfall in their use, and give what they
  get once it's good, in their terms, never how to make it, as how to mend is the maker's call.
- `about` names the section where the people need the state. Look for it there, as that is where
  they look.
