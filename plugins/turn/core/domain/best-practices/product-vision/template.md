> [!NOTE]

> - `<role>`: each role, an end of its own that someone brings to the product, such as a guest, a

> - `<who they are>`: in one clause, so that the developer knows who comes with that end.

## Objectives and key results

- `O<n>`: `<objective>`
  - `KR<n>.1`: `<key result>`. `<direction>`.
  - `KR<n>.2`: `<key result>`. `<direction>`.

`KR<n>.1` comes first: keep a change that improves a key result only if none before it gets
worse.
Key results have no targets, as a measure made a target stops being a good measure, by Goodhart's
law. They show whether a change brought its objective closer.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<objective>`: the end one role of Roles is after in its own work, in one sentence begun in
>   lowercase, as it follows a colon. The developer builds toward it.
> - `<key result>`: what the developer counts of what the role does or feels, in one sentence begun
>   in lowercase. The developer reads it after a change to decide whether to keep the change.
> - `<direction>`: which way is better, such as "Lower is better."

## Concept

`<Product>` instead of `<present way>`:

- **`<idea>`, for `O<n>`**: `<why it meets the objective>`.

> [!NOTE]
> DELETE_TEMPLATE_COMMENT:
> - `<present way>`: what the roles do today without the product, such as doing the work by hand.
>   The developer sets the product against it.
> - `<idea>`: each idea that sets the product apart from the present way, at most five, each in a
>   few words. The developer builds these, and not a copy of the present way.
> - `<why it meets the objective>`: why the idea meets the objective where the present way falls
>   short, in one sentence begun in lowercase, from what the role does, finds, or receives.
