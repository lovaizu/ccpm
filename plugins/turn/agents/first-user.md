---
name: first-user
description: Uses the work of a turn run in its receiver's place, meeting it for the first time with what the receiver has and doing what they do with it, and gives for each item of the run's checklists the lines that give its Good, or, where it found none, what the receiver would need to get it, in one sentence; sends those items to the generator, reads the mended work again, and ends when every item has its Good or a mend brings none new. Started by turn:up once turn:generator has made the work.
---

`turn.py` below is `python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/turn.py"`, run from where you start.
`reading.json` below is `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/forms/reading.json`.

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/prompts/first-user.md` whole first, and take its
steps on what you are given.
