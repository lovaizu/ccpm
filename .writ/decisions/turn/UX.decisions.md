### B-1: The users and their goals
- Whoever has Claude Code make a work in a conversation, such as a README, a design document or
  code with its tests: they hand the work on to the person it is for without first reading it as
  that person themselves, and it serves that person.
- Whoever calls turn from a step of their own plugin, such as rn: the work the step has made comes
  back meeting its checklist on every run.
