# Prompt

The reader is the AI that acts by following the prompt. When it finishes, it can choose for itself a way of acting that achieves the purpose, even in situations the writer did not foresee.

- Can the AI act from who it is and what it is for, even where no step reaches, with every sentence something to do?

    When the AI knows who it is before it reads what to do, every order has someone to carry it out: who it reads as, what it decides itself, and what it returns to the requester instead. A purpose that gives the benefit to the product's user and why it is needed, even for a role that works for another role, lets it choose what fits where the steps do not reach. Steps kept to rules that break things when they drift and cannot be derived, such as the format of a handoff, leave everything else to that choice; take a step away, and if the AI would still act rightly, it is not a step. A purpose that ends at the handoff asks only for what the requester can check, and one that summarises the steps sends the AI back to following them. Steps are followed exactly, so a flow of work copied into numbered steps, or a step phrased as a habit such as "confirm with the user", overrides the purpose where the two disagree. The AI also acts on what was meant only as explanation, such as things that do not exist, how decisions came about, or a summary of a document it is told to read, which drifts from its source each time that document is refined.

- Can a maintainer tell, for each part of a prompt and each mechanism it relies on, which feature in the design it serves?

    A maintainer who can trace each part and each mechanism, such as an agent definition, a hook or a subagent launch, to a feature can tell what changing it costs. One that traces to nothing was chosen with no ground anyone can check and is kept by habit; either it is left out, or the design is changed first.

- Does each role get everything its work needs from someone who writes it, and nothing it must not see?

    When every output one role leaves is read by the next, everything a role reads is written by someone, and each role knows what it must not see and why, nothing falls through between roles and each keeps the view it is there for. A role told another will see its output stops dealing with it, and if no one reads it, it is lost. A checking role handed the producing role's reasons, or free to fetch git history, the discussion or earlier versions, fills the work's gaps with them and misses where the reader trips; the tools it needs reach those too, so tools alone cannot keep it out.

- Does the checking role spend its judgment on whether the reader gets the benefit, and return what the requester fixes by?

    Only a role that did not produce the work can read it without knowing the discussion, so its attention goes to whether the reader gets the benefit they would choose the work for. It reads the same essentials the producing role aimed for, as questions, and returns for each a Good that says what the reader gains, which a fix must not take away, and a More that says what the reader struggles with, both with evidence the requester can check, such as path:line or a command and its output. A Good that says only "easy to understand" leaves whoever fixes it not knowing what to keep.

- Can the requester decide its next move itself by weighing the check's results against the purpose, and always bring the cycle of evaluating and fixing to an end?

    With judgment in one place that knows the purpose, each More is fixed, let go or handed to a person, each Good whose evidence does not hold is treated as a More, and the cycle ends as either settled or cannot proceed. Acted on literally, a check's results redo even the parts that serve the purpose, and each new evaluation brings new non-essential remarks.

- Can a person decide each call with ease, from what the AI proposes to do next toward the purpose, why, and its grounds?

    A call that proposes the next move toward the purpose with its reason, and, where there is a finished work, the final Good and More for each question as grounds, lets the person say yes or no and decide from the call, reading the work whenever they want. Asked only what the conversation, the documents handed over and the repository do not already tell, one decision at a time so each answer can shape the next, they answer with ease. Asked several things at once, or handed remarks and fixes from along the way, they work out what an earlier answer already settled or which remarks still apply.
