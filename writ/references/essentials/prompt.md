# Prompt

The reader is the AI that acts by following the prompt. When it finishes, it can choose for itself a way of acting that achieves the purpose, even in situations the writer did not foresee. A maintainer also reads it, to change it without losing what it is for. The questions on roles, checking and turning to a person apply where the prompt is part of a product that has them.

- Can the AI act from who it is and what it is for, even where no step reaches?

    Knowing who it is before it reads what to do, and a purpose that gives the benefit to the product's user and why it is needed, the AI chooses what fits where the steps do not reach. Steps are then only rules that break things when they drift and cannot be derived, such as the format of a handoff; take a step away, and if the AI would still act rightly, it was not needed. Steps are followed exactly, so a flow of work copied into them, a purpose that only summarises them, or a sentence meant only as explanation is acted on even where it goes against the purpose.

- Can a maintainer tell, for each part of a prompt and each mechanism it relies on, which feature in the design it serves?

    A maintainer who can trace each part and each mechanism, such as an agent definition, a hook or a subagent launch, to a feature can tell what changing it costs. One that traces to nothing has no ground anyone can check, and is kept by habit.

- Does each role get everything its work needs from someone who writes it, and nothing it must not see?

    When every output one role leaves is read by the next, everything a role reads is written by someone, and each role knows what it must not see and why, nothing falls through between roles and each keeps the view it is there for. A checking role handed the producing role's reasons, or free to fetch git history, the discussion or earlier versions, fills the work's gaps with them and misses where the reader trips; the tools it needs reach those too, so tools alone cannot keep it out.

- Does the checking role spend its judgment on whether the reader gets the benefit, and return what the requester fixes by?

    Only a role that did not produce the work can read it without knowing the discussion, so its attention goes to whether the reader gets the benefit they would choose the work for. It returns, for each question, a Good that says what the reader gains, which a fix must not take away, and a More that says what the reader struggles with, both with evidence the requester can check. A Good that says only "easy to understand" leaves whoever fixes it not knowing what to keep.

- Can the requester decide its next move itself by weighing the check's results against the purpose, and always bring the cycle of evaluating and fixing to an end?

    With judgment in one place that knows the purpose, the requester weighs each More and the evidence of each Good against the purpose, and the cycle ends, either with the work settled or with the reason it cannot go on. Acted on literally, a check's results redo even the parts that serve the purpose, and each new evaluation brings new non-essential remarks.

- When the AI turns to a person, can the person decide with ease?

    Given what the AI proposes to do next toward the purpose, why, and its grounds, such as the final Good and More for each question, the person can say yes or no from that alone and read the work whenever they want. Asked only what the conversation, the documents handed over and the repository do not already tell, and asked each thing after the answers that could change it, they answer with ease. Remarks and fixes from along the way leave them working out which still apply.
