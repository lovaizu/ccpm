# Making a checklist

A checklist for a kind writ has none for is made first, as a document of its own, since the
document's open points rest on it; then the request goes on with it.

1. Its kind is `checklist`, its reader whoever writes or judges documents of the kind, and its use
   to write or judge them to their purpose; what they must be able to do is to tell whom such a
   document is for and what that person can then do, and to answer each item yes or no as anyone
   else would.
2. Go through steps 3 to 12 of the skill for it, with its path `.writ/checklists/<kind>.md`, where
   `<kind>` is the kind's name as step 3 named it, such as `release-notes`. Its first open point is
   the kind's purpose, who reads such a document and what they can then do, since every item is
   drawn from it. When a plugin's step called you, its points go back to the step as any others.
3. Once the checklist is written, go on with the document's request at step 3, where
   `up.py checklists <kind>` now finds the checklist at that path.
