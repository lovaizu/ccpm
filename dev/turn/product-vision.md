# Turn Product Vision

## Roles

- **user**: one who asks Turn for a document or code, such as a README, for the people it is for.

## OKRs

- O1: The user gets work with which the people it is for achieve what they are after.
  - KR1.1: Of the works the user asks for, the share that the people it is for could not use for
    what they are after as handed over. Lower is better.
  - KR1.2: The time from the user's request to that work, the user's own time answering Turn
    included. Shorter is better.

KR1.1 comes first: a change that improves KR1.2 is kept only if KR1.1 does not get worse. Key
results have no targets, as a measure made a target stops being a good measure (Goodhart's
law). They show whether a change brought its objective closer.

## Concept

Turn instead of asking an AI in one prompt, reading its draft and fixing it:

- **Made from a best practice**: the work is built toward what the people it is for are after, by
  the best practice of its kind, not mapped from similar text by an AI starting from scratch (O1).
- **Checked by purpose and intent**: Turn checks whether the people the work is for get what they
  are after, not whether the work follows the best practice, so the user is handed no findings
  off the purpose to sort (O1).
- **Every intent drawn out**: the user's purpose and intent for the work are drawn out point by
  point before it is made, so the work rests on what they mean, not on guesses (O1).
- **Checked in use**: the work is tried as the people it is for would use it, so it serves them in
  use, not only on reading (O1).
- **Seeks the good**: the work is made good from its root, not patched fault by fault, so it
  carries none of the noise that added fixes bring (O1).
