# Product vision of rn

## Roles

- **user**: one who asks rn for a deliverable, such as a document, code or a plugin, for the people
  it's for.

## Objectives and key results

- O1: the user has each deliverable they mean made, with which the people it's for achieve what
  they're after.
  - KR1.1: the share of the deliverables rn hands over that the people they're for couldn't use
    for what they're after. Lower is better.
  - KR1.2: the time from the user's request to the deliverable, the user's own time answering rn
    included. Shorter is better.

KR1.1 comes first: keep a change that improves a key result only if none before it
gets worse.
Key results have no targets, as a measure made a target stops being a good measure, by Goodhart's
law. They show whether a change brought its objective closer.

## Concept

rn instead of asking an AI in one prompt, reading its draft and fixing it:

- **The vital few only, for O1**: rn keeps in the design tree only the decisions that move the
  goal, and makes and improves each deliverable from them by its kind's best practice, so no
  trivial finding piles up on either.
- **Judged by its people, for O1**: rn judges each deliverable only by whether its people get what
  they're after.
- **Only the user's points asked, for O1**: rn asks one point at a time, only what the purpose and
  the best practices leave open.
- **Planned back from the goal, replanned as it goes, for O1**: rn makes the deliverables from the
  first and plans again from each result.
- **Every step, every run, for O1**: rn keeps each step on every run and goes on where the work
  stopped.
